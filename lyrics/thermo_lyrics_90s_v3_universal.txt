# Thermodynamics v3 UNIVERSAL: one sheet for all genres, 90 s. Shortened from v2 (281 -> ~210 syllables), rhyming kept.
# Changes vs v2: opens on the chorus (the hook), the middle chorus and the separate hook are removed,
# "Laws deal with energy changes" merged into the macroscopic line, and "under certain conditions" /
# "one form into another" moved into Verse 1. All unique facts finish before the final chorus.
[Chorus]
Thermodynamics! Study of these transformations!
Not how, not at what rate,
Based on initial state, final state!

[Verse 1]
Chemical energy, stored by molecules,
Methane, cooking gas or coal: fuel!
Fuel burns in air? Heat! Reactions, chemical!
In an engine? Work, mechanical!
Galvanic cell, like dry cell? Energy, electrical!
Interrelated forms, under certain conditions,
From one form into another: transformations!

[Instrumental]

[Verse 2]
Laws deal with energy changes: macroscopic,
A large number of molecules,
Rather than microscopic: a few molecules!

[Bridge]
Laws apply only when a system is in equilibrium,
Or moves, state to state, equilibrium to equilibrium!
Pressure, temperature, in equilibrium:
Do not change with time!

[Final Chorus]
Thermodynamics! Study of these transformations!
Not how, not at what rate,
Based on initial state, final state!
