[Hook]
Thermodynamics! Energy transformations!
Forms of energy, from one form into another!

[Verse 1]
Chemical energy, stored by molecules,
Fuel burns in air? Heat from chemical reactions!
Methane, cooking gas or coal!
In an engine? Mechanical work!
Galvanic cell, like dry cell? Electrical energy!

[Pre-Chorus]
Forms of energy, interrelated,
Under certain conditions, transformed!

[Chorus]
Thermodynamics! The study of these transformations!
Not how, not at what rate: initial and final state!

[Instrumental]

[Verse 2]
Laws deal with energy changes:
Macroscopic systems? A large number of molecules!
Rather than microscopic? A few molecules!
Initial, final states: a system undergoing the change!

[Chorus]
Thermodynamics! The study of these transformations!
Not how, not at what rate: initial and final state!

[Bridge]
Laws apply only when a system is in equilibrium,
Or moves from one equilibrium state to another!
In equilibrium, pressure and temperature
Do not change with time!

[Final Chorus]
Thermodynamics! The study of these transformations!
Not how, not at what rate: initial and final state!
