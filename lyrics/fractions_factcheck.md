## C. Notes for the client (about the theory text)
1. **"slice an apple into two parts, then each part … equal to 1/2"**: each part is ½ only if the two parts are **equal**. The lyric sings "two **equal** parts" ("equal" appears elsewhere in the theory) so the song is correct; the theory text should add "equal".
2. **"Three people, four slices"** is an unexplained example (it doesn't divide evenly). Sung as written; the client may want to finish the thought or drop it.
3. **"In this article, we are going to learn … types of fractions, conversion from fractions to decimals, and many solved examples"** describes content that isn't in this theory. It isn't sung. If the client wants those topics, they need their own theory text.
