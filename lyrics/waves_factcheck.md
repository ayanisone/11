## C. Notes for the client (theory text, not lyric errors)
1. **The pond example is printed under "Matter Waves"**, but a stone in a pond makes a water (mechanical) wave. The lyric keeps the line but sings it right after "water waves" and before "Matter waves?", so it isn't presented as a matter-wave example.
2. **Electromagnetic waves "need no object medium"** reads oddly in the theory. The lyric sings "Not any medium!", which keeps the meaning.
3. Amplitude "represents the energy": strictly, energy grows with amplitude **squared**. The theory's wording ("directly related", "greater amplitude, greater energy") is kept and is fine for this level.
