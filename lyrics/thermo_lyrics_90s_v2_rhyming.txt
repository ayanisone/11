[Hook]
Thermodynamics! Energy transformations!
From one form into another, under certain conditions!

[Verse 1]
Chemical energy, stored by molecules,
Methane, cooking gas or coal: fuel!
Fuel burns in air? Heat! Reactions, chemical!
Fuel burns in an engine? Work, mechanical!
Galvanic cell, like dry cell? Energy, electrical!

[Pre-Chorus]
Forms of energy, interrelated, transformed,
From one form into another form!

[Chorus]
Thermodynamics! Study of these transformations!
Not how, not at what rate,
Based on initial state, final state!

[Instrumental]

[Verse 2]
Laws deal with energy changes
Of a system undergoing the change!
Macroscopic? A large number of molecules!
Rather than microscopic? A few molecules!

[Chorus]
Thermodynamics! Study of these transformations!
Not how, not at what rate,
Based on initial state, final state!

[Bridge]
Laws apply only when a system is in equilibrium,
Or moves, state to state, equilibrium to equilibrium!
Pressure, temperature, in equilibrium:
Do not change with time!

[Final Chorus]
Thermodynamics! Study of these transformations!
Not how, not at what rate,
Based on initial state, final state!
