[Hook]
Newton's three laws of motion!
[First Law]
First law, law of inertia:
In motion, continues in motion,
At rest, remains at rest,
Unless an unbalanced force!
[Second Law]
Second law, mathematically expressed:
F equals m a, F is force,
m is mass, a is acceleration,
Of classical mechanics, the foundation!
[Chorus: Third Law]
Action, reaction!
For every action, an equal and opposite reaction,
Equal in magnitude, opposite in direction,
Two forces, two different objects:
Action, reaction!
